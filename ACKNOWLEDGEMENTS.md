# Acknowledgements and design lineage

CardiBench's living discovery/catalog layer was independently implemented for the Virelion HeartTwin architecture after reviewing public benchmark-discovery infrastructure. Benchmark Radar by Koutian Wu and contributors motivated general patterns including immutable source observations, explicit source health, evidence/catalog separation, explainable search, and visible missing evidence.

Benchmark Radar software is MIT licensed, while its report/editorial content and collected third-party material have separate terms. CardiBench does not vendor Benchmark Radar's corpus, editorial summaries, graphics, or paper text.

Upstream reference: https://github.com/ktwu01/benchmark-radar
