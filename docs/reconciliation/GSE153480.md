## Experimental unit

From STAR Methods and Figure 1 legend:

> For each scRNA-seq sample, **8–12 ventricular samples collected from the same litter were pooled together**.  
> **n = 8–12 animals** were used for tissue collection. **n = 1 sequencing library was generated for each time point and condition.**

Therefore the independent biological unit for CardiBench is the **library/pool**, not an individual mouse. No individual animal IDs exist in GEO or the paper; none were invented.

## Caveats (do not ignore)

1. Libraries are **pools** of 8–12 animals. Do not treat single cells as independent biological replicates.
2. P1 (regenerative) and P8 (non-regenerative) are biologically distinct; prefer stratum-specific analysis or explicit age modelling.
3. Expression matrices are **not** redistributed; only metadata and locked split manifests.
4. This promotion advances the dataset from `metadata_verified_candidate` toward `benchmark_ready` / `locked` for library-level MI-vs-reference tasks.
